Public descriptions characterize Biogax as a modern clean-tech / renewable-energy template with clean design, strong visuals, optimized messaging, fully responsive behavior, and pages structured around Home, About, Services, Projects, Project Detail, Blog, Contact, legal pages, and 404.

Implementation direction for Open House Tracker: translate that clean-tech marketing language into a calm, optimistic property-operations workspace. Use a lighter organic palette than the current black/lime command center, generous rounded cards, soft off-white canvas, deep botanical green typography, fresh lime/leaf accents, strong visual hero framing, and clear card-based project groupings. Preserve all existing functional sections: dashboard, inventory, sourced, sold, ledger history, upload/OCR, flexible NCR region/zone/micro-zone filters, Excel downloads, reset, profile, and sign-out.


## Direct Preview Findings

The public preview uses a full-bleed green landscape hero image with a dark natural overlay. Navigation sits directly over the image in white, with a small Biogax wordmark at left, centered links, and a rounded pale-lime primary CTA at right. The hero centers a compact rounded eyebrow label, a large centered white headline, two rounded CTA buttons, a horizontal line of three benefits, and a three-column numeric stat row near the bottom of the image. The following section switches to a clean white canvas with muted gray copy and another rounded CTA, creating a high-contrast nature-to-white rhythm. The visual language is optimistic and organic rather than dark editorial: large scenic imagery, softened corners, green/lime accents, generous white space, and card-based content hierarchy.

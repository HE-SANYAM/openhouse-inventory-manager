User-requested feature additions tied to this reference:

- Accept PDF files and supported report images for OCR review.
- Divide inventory through flexible NCR market-region, zone, and micro-zone fields rather than fixed enums.
- Starter examples include Gurgaon (SPR, Dwarka Expressway, New Gurgaon), Noida (Noida Extension, Gaur City 1 & Gaur City 2, sector groupings, Noida Expressway, Central Noida), and Ghaziabad (Crossing Republik, Raj Nagar Extension, NH-24), while allowing future labels.
